# Recovered old design bible — authority map

Recovered 2026-10-05 from **Eurorack Incremental — Consolidated Game Design Bible**.

This is historical design evidence, not blanket authority over the current game.
The current governing design and accepted G1 decisions supersede old mechanics where
they conflict. This document exists so the project can recover its world, tone and
content identity without accidentally restoring obsolete pressure systems.

## What the old bible establishes

### Preserve as project identity

- **Adventure first, incremental second.** The original project was never meant to
  be an endless idle treadmill.
- The player's grandfather disappeared after his wall-sized modular crossed from
  instrument into a reality machine. Rebuilding the rack also rebuilds the path
  between realities and may eventually bring him home.
- The setting is a compact, strange, near-future Helsinki rather than an enormous
  open world.
- Eurorack is the prized central object: instrument, collection, progression
  object and story engine.
- Humour is dry and absurd, while real modular terminology and functions remain
  recognisable. The joke is not that synthesis itself is stupid.
- The game should remain the **relaxed adventurous counterpart** to the work-pressure
  satire found in profession ideas. It must not become a workplace-stress simulator.
- Useful world locations already exist in the old design vocabulary: Home,
  Workshop/module shop, city streets, repair/DIY workshop, black market,
  modular festival / ModCon and second-hand market.
- Real Eurorack knowledge was always intended to matter. Module functions, ports
  and balanced rack recipes were planned as later depth rather than arbitrary card
  flavour.
- Fictionalised manufacturers and original module identities are worth preserving.
- Kalevala / Sampo / Aino material is later escalation, not the opening identity.

### Preserve selectively as content vocabulary

Old jobs, bosses, curses, repair ideas, city events and manufacturers are a bank
of fiction and encounter concepts. They are not automatically restored as systems.
A boss name can become a strange commission, festival encounter or project without
restoring autobattle. A damaged module can become interesting audible behaviour
without restoring punitive weekly contamination.

## Superseded mechanics

The following belonged to the earlier incremental/card prototype and no longer
govern the future game:

- three Action Points per day and mandatory seven-day pacing;
- job grind as the main source of progression;
- seven-card numeric starter decks, draw-three voltage scoring and global card values;
- weekly forced deck contamination and curse pressure;
- rarity/value ladders as global power progression;
- automatic rack autobattle bosses and score-gate victory;
- mandatory daily/weekly clocks;
- menu-only-first as a current production requirement;
- fake sound identity via one loop/sample per module;
- treating realistic Audio/CV/Gate patching as a distant optional experiment.

Those ideas remain archaeology. They must not leak back into the persistent
Studio / Project / Archive design by implementation accident.

## Relationship to current governing design

The current game keeps the old project's strongest premise but changes the
mechanical centre:

**PATCH → LISTEN → CHANGE → DISCOVER → CAPTURE / PERFORM**

The studio persists. Projects are finite. Musical quality is not graded. Noise,
silence, instability and feedback are valid. Opportunities should invite curiosity
rather than constantly demand attention.

The implemented G1-P six-module slice remains useful as a state/persistence test,
but it is not the intended full-game starter experience. Post-G1 direction is to
begin with a **working, dismantlable starter rack / patch template**, then let the
adventure introduce stranger relationships and modules.

## Recovered north-star story

The old bible's durable story idea can be summarized as:

> Rebuild Grandfather's reality-breaking Eurorack, explore the fractured city around
> it, and eventually discover whether the impossible instrument can bring him home.

The exact campaign structure, ending and later mythology remain future authored
content, not implementation requirements for the next milestone.
